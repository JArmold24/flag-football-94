Privacy Policy

Flag Football '94 - effective October 29, 2026

Flag Football '94 is a single-player football game. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings, Superstar careers, Franchise leagues, custom plays and Hall of Fame are saved only on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game posts your retired Superstar's legacy score to a Game Center leaderboard and unlocks Game Center achievements. Game Center is run by Apple; your Game Center nickname and scores are handled under Apple's privacy policy. You can play without Game Center, and you can turn it off at any time in the Settings app.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
