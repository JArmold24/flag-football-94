Common questions

  • What is free, and what does the Full Game add? The free download includes one exhibition game. The Full Game, a one-time in-app purchase, unlocks Superstar Mode, Franchise Mode, unlimited exhibition games, Practice, the playbook editor, rosters and the Hall of Fame. No ads, no subscription.
  • I bought the Full Game - how do I get it on a new device? Open Main Menu > Unlock Full Game > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.
  • How do I throw? Tap the receiver you want on the field. A tap throws a lob, holding throws a bullet. You can switch to receiver windows in Options > Passing.
  • Where are my saves? On your device. Careers, franchises and settings are kept between launches and updates; deleting the app removes them.
  • The leaderboard? Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app. Retire a Superstar and his legacy score is posted to the Hall of Fame leaderboard.
  • No sound? Check the ring/silent switch and the volume, and Options > Music / Sound Effects.
  • It got too hard? On DYNAMIC difficulty the game climbs a level with every win. Options > Dynamic Level lets you set it back.
